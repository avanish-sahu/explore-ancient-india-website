Bharatanatyam
  • 1.Bharatanatyam is one of the oldest and most respected classical dance forms of India. It originated in the temples of Tamil Nadu, particularly around Chennai, and has a history of more than 2,000 years. This dance form combines rhythmic footwork, expressive gestures, facial expressions, and storytelling to convey spiritual ideas and cultural traditions.

  • 2.Bharatanatyam is not only a dance but also a form of devotion and storytelling. Traditionally, it was performed in temples by dancers known as Devadasis, who dedicated their art to worshipping the gods. The dance expresses stories from Hindu scriptures and epics and communicates emotions through movements and expressions.

  • Origin and Historical Background :

  • 1.The roots of Bharatanatyam can be traced to the ancient treatise Natya Shastra, written by the sage Bharata Muni around 200 BCE to 200 CE. This text describes the principles of dance, drama, music, stage design, gestures, and emotions.

  • 2.In ancient times, Bharatanatyam was performed in temples as a sacred ritual. During the colonial period in the 19th century, the dance tradition declined. However, in the 20th century it was revived by cultural reformers and artists who preserved and promoted it as a classical stage art.

  • Meaning of the Word Bharatanatyam :

  • The name Bharatanatyam is often explained using three Sanskrit words:

  • 1.Bha – Bhava (emotion and expression)

  • 2.Ra – Raga (melody and music)

  • 3.Ta – Tala (rhythm and timing)

  • 4.Natyam – Dance or dramatic performance

  • Together, these elements represent the complete integration of emotion, music, rhythm, and movement in classical dance.

  • Important Elements of Bharatanatyam

  • 1. Nritta (Pure Dance)
  • Nritta refers to the pure rhythmic movements of the dance without any storytelling. It focuses on footwork, patterns, and body movements that match the rhythm of the music.

  • 2. Nritya (Expressive Dance)

  • Nritya combines dance movements with expressions and gestures to communicate emotions and stories.

  • 3. Natya (Dramatic Representation)

  • Natya refers to the dramatic aspect of dance, where performers act out stories from mythology and religious texts.

  • Abhinaya (Expression in Dance)

  • An essential part of Bharatanatyam is Abhinaya, which means expressing emotions and ideas through gestures and expressions. There are four types of Abhinaya:

  • 1.Angika – Expression through body movements and gestures

  • 2.Vachika – Expression through speech or song

  • 3.Aharya – Expression through costumes and makeup

  • 4.Sattvika – Expression through inner emotions

  • These elements help the dancer convey the meaning of the story to the audience.

  • Mudras and Gestures:

  • Bharatanatyam uses hand gestures called mudras to communicate ideas and actions. Mudras represent objects, emotions, or characters. Through combinations of mudras and facial expressions, dancers narrate stories from sacred texts.

  • Costume and Appearance:

  • The Bharatanatyam costume is colorful and designed to highlight the dancer’s movements. Key elements include:

  • A pleated silk costume that opens during leg movements

  • Traditional temple jewelry :

  • 1. Ankle bells (Ghungroo) that emphasize rhythm

  • 2. Decorative makeup and head ornaments

  • 3. These elements enhance the visual beauty of the performance.

  • Music and Accompaniment

  • Bharatanatyam performances are accompanied by classical Carnatic music. The musicians typically include:

  • 1. A vocalist

  • 2. A mridangam (drum) player

  • 3. A violin or flute player

  • 4. A nattuvanar, who conducts the performance and recites rhythmic syllables

  • 5.The coordination between the dancer and musicians creates a powerful artistic experience.

  • Themes and Stories:

  • Most Bharatanatyam performances depict stories from famous Indian scriptures and epics such as the Ramayana and the Mahabharata. Dancers portray gods, heroes, and mythological characters through expressive storytelling.

  • Common themes include:

  • 1. Devotion to gods such as Shiva, Vishnu, and Krishna

  • 2. Love and devotion between the devotee and the divine

  • 3. Moral lessons and spiritual ideas

  • Importance of Bharatanatyam

  • 1.Bharatanatyam plays a significant role in preserving Indian culture, mythology, and spiritual traditions. It is recognized worldwide as a classical dance form that reflects India’s artistic heritage.

  • 2.The dance also helps develop discipline, body coordination, emotional expression, and appreciation for classical music and culture. Today Bharatanatyam is performed on stages across the world and continues to inspire new generations of dancers.
